# Project overview

## Purpose

Morrow is a chess-learning prototype for players who want to understand why a move works and recognize recurring decision patterns.

## Audience

Chess learners who want guided practice and reflection rather than a standalone engine evaluation.

## Capabilities

- A monochrome public landing page with a scroll-driven ASCII pawn and an interactive board demo.
- A workspace dashboard, practice board, game review, and progress screens.
- Legal move handling through `chess.js` and deterministic mentor notes.
- Local persistence of PGN/FEN practice sessions in SQLite.

## Technology

Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, `chess.js`, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Node's built-in SQLite API. Node.js 24 or newer is required.

## Status and limits

Active private prototype. There is no authentication, chess engine, cloud database, or automated behavioral test suite. The mentor notes are rule-based and are not generated by an AI model.
